# Security policy

If you find a security problem in Zrzur, please write to **security@zrzur.com**. Do not open a public issue.

- Describe what you found and how to reproduce it. Do not include other people's personal data.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish it.
- Do not test against other people's accounts or data, and do not disrupt the service.

We will confirm receipt and tell you what we decide.

Zrzur is not affiliated with Stoat or Revolt. Please do not send Zrzur reports to the Stoat team.
