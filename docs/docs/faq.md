# Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for the **Zarzour** project. This page does **not** republish Stoat platform policies, verification procedures, or Stoat support contacts as if they applied to Zarzour.

**Last update:** 2026-10-20

<details>
  <summary>What is Zarzour?</summary>

  Zarzour (زرزور) is an independent open-source communication platform. Product generation **Zarzour 26**. Website target: [zarzour.cc](https://zarzour.cc) (DNS/TLS must be verified by the owner before treating it as live).

  Zarzour is derived from the open-source [Stoat](https://github.com/stoatchat) project (formerly Revolt). Stoat does not sponsor or endorse Zarzour.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Where is the source?</summary>

  Planned independent repositories under [github.com/zrzur](https://github.com/zrzur): `backend`, `web`, `desktop`, and private `infrastructure`. Publication is owner-controlled and may still be pending.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>How do I self-host?</summary>

  Use the Zarzour infrastructure Compose templates and the component READMEs. You must comply with the AGPL (and any crate-specific licenses): publish corresponding source for production modifications, and preserve required copyright/attribution notices.

  Compatibility config filename `Revolt.toml` and related protocol identifiers may remain for toolchain compatibility; they are not a claim of Stoat branding.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Does Zarzour have federation?</summary>

  Federation is **not** in the current Zarzour 26 scope without separate written approval.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Server verification / badges / Stoat support articles?</summary>

  Stoat’s verification, badge, and knowledge-base processes apply to the Stoat platform only. Zarzour does not currently publish equivalent official policies or support URLs. Do not email Stoat contacts for Zarzour issues.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Where is historical Stoat / Revolt endpoint information?</summary>

  See [Stoat migration guide (historical)](./developers/stoat-migration-guide.md) for upstream Revolt→Stoat URL history. Those hosts are **not** Zarzour defaults.
</details>
